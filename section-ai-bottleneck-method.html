Step 1

Map Your Full Production Chain

List every stage end-to-end. For each stage note: who does it, how long it takes, what it blocks downstream.

A firm's output depends on the joint quality of many interconnected, complementary activities.
Key Principle

The critical test: “If I made this stage instant, would overall output actually increase?”

Step 2

Find the Binding Bottleneck(s)

The bottleneck is the constraint that actually limits throughput. Classic signs:

📦 Work piles up
⏳ Downstream waits
🐢 Slowest step
💎 Scarcest resource
Real-World Examples
📋
Trade Quality Control
Inspector throughput as the binding constraint

In trade quality control, the bottleneck is inspector throughput. Products queue for inspection while downstream shipping and fulfillment wait idle. AI vision systems can inspect at 10× human speed, but only if the bottleneck doesn't just shift to documentation or approval workflows.

Bottleneck: Inspector throughput limits the entire trade pipeline.
ManufacturingQC
🚑
Medical Transport
Dispatcher capacity as the binding constraint

Medical transport services are often constrained by dispatcher capacity — a single human routing dozens of vehicles in real time. AI-powered dispatch can optimize routes and reduce response times, but the true gains come only when downstream stages (driver availability, patient readiness) can keep pace.

Bottleneck: Dispatcher capacity limits patient throughput and response times.
HealthcareLogistics
🔧
Field Services
Communication overhead as the binding constraint

Field service organizations lose enormous time to communication overhead — technicians calling dispatch, waiting for parts information, writing reports. AI can automate scheduling, surface repair guides in real time, and auto-generate reports — but the chain only speeds up if parts logistics and customer scheduling also improve.

Bottleneck: Communication overhead between field, dispatch, and back-office.

Step 3

Test AI Capability at That Bottleneck

Three rules for testing AI at the constraint:

1
Don't guess — prototype and test
Theoretical capability means nothing. Run a real pilot with real data at the actual bottleneck stage.
2
Look at analogies from similar firms
Find peers who have already tried AI at a comparable bottleneck. Their results tell you more than any vendor demo.
3
Test the full chain, not just one step
Measure end-to-end throughput, not just the stage you automated.
⚠ “Partial automation preserves the bottleneck”
Critical Insight

The Bottleneck Chase

When activities within the firm are tightly coupled, the value of applying AI to one activity depends on whether adjacent activities also adjust.
The Complementarity Principle

Fixing one bottleneck just moves it. The method:

Step 1
Map the chain
Identify every stage in your production process and its dependencies.
Step 2
Find the bottleneck
Locate the single constraint that limits overall throughput today.
Step 3
Where does it move if AI fixes it?
Once this constraint is eliminated, which stage becomes the new bottleneck?
Step 4
Can AI handle that one too?
Evaluate whether AI can also address the next constraint in the chain.
Step 5
Compounding or diminishing?
✅
Yes = Compounding Gains
AI can chase bottleneck through multiple stages
⛔
No = Modest Gains
New bottleneck is beyond AI's reach
Concrete Example

The Prototyping Case: Chasing Through 3 Stages

Watch how the bottleneck migrates through the chain — and why the biggest gains come from following it.

✔
Stage 1 — Solved
Building Prototypes
Old bottleneck: Physical prototyping took weeks. Teams waited for models before testing anything.
AI fix: Generative design and rapid prototyping tools reduced build time from weeks to hours.
✅ AI handles this
✔
Stage 2 — Solved
Customer Feedback
Bottleneck moved here: Now that prototypes were fast, gathering customer feedback became the constraint.
AI fix: Automated testing panels, sentiment analysis, and rapid A/B testing tools accelerated feedback cycles.
✅ AI handles this too
⚠
Stage 3 — Current Constraint
Identifying What Customers Want
Bottleneck landed here: Speed without direction. You can build and test fast, but are you building the right thing?
⚠ If unsolved: “build the wrong product faster”
6×
Revenue gains came from chasing the bottleneck through 2–3 stages, not from fixing just one. Each stage AI solved unlocked the next — compounding the impact across the entire chain.
